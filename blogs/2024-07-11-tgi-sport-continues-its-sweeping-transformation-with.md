TGI Sport, the global leader in sports technology and media rights, announced an agreement to acquire Supponor, the leading live media virtual advertising specialist, with proprietary technologies and software that delivers virtual ads across thousands of live sports broadcasts worldwide. It will join an impressive portfolio of capabilities to round out a best-in-class virtual ad tech practice with incomparable capabilities in a rapidly growing sector. The deal is scheduled to close mid-summer and is the latest in a series of moves that have transformed the company.
